Free Split Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Free Split keeps your data on your iPhone. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Sign in

You can sign in with Apple or Google. We only use your name and email to open your account on this phone. Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email from us. Google Sign-In is not used for ads.

Optional access

Contacts, photos, Bluetooth, and local network are used only when you add friends, pick a group photo, or sync a split with a nearby phone. You can deny those permissions.

Splits

Groups, expenses, and payments stay on this device. If you sync with a friend nearby or share a file, that copy lives on their phone too.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. That removes your sign-in and local splits from this iPhone.
