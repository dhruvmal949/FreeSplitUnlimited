Free Split Support

Free Split helps friends track shared expenses on iPhone and iPad.

Sign in

Use Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google. Email and password sign-in is not offered yet. App Review: please use Sign in with Apple. There is no username or password demo account.

Try every feature

After you sign in, open Groups and tap Create Group. Add people, an expense, and a payment. Friends holds one-to-one splits. Settings lets you sign out or delete your account.
