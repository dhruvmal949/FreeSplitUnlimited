Free Split Support

Free Split helps friends track shared expenses on iPhone.

Sign in

Use Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google. Your splits stay with that account on this iPhone.

Groups and friends

Groups are shared trips and bills. Friends holds one-to-one splits.

Expenses

Open a group or friend split and tap Add expense. Choose who paid and how to split it.

Settle up

Use Settle up when someone pays the other back. Balances only lists people who still owe or are owed.

Delete

Swipe left on a group, friend, or notification to remove it. To remove your account, open Settings and tap Delete Account.
